Leaderboard

Public leaderboard of the MetaFine Open Competition. Scores follow the evaluation protocol described in COMPETITION.md. The π0 baseline is listed for reference and does not compete for awards.

Note to participants: after registering, please watch for the registration confirmation email — it contains your submission instructions. To submit, create a private Hugging Face model repo containing your policy and add the organizer account as a collaborator. Submitted repos are scanned daily; a submission enters the evaluation queue whenever the scan detects that it is flagged for evaluation, and the results are posted here. Full submission details are in the Submission section of COMPETITION.md.
